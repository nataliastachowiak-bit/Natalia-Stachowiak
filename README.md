## Featured case study — ResourceLoop AI

**Circular renovation · Scan-to-Decision · AI-assisted decision support**

**Problem:** Decisions on keeping, repairing, reusing, refurbishing, remanufacturing or recycling building components are difficult when condition, material and compliance information is scattered or missing.

**Product approach:** Combine component records, evidence quality, technical/environmental/economic criteria and transparent scenario comparison. A mobile photo-based capture workflow helps structure observations and identify missing information.

**MVP demonstrator:** An aluminium façade component is the deliberately narrow use case. The scan workflow can document visible characteristics, flag uncertainty and prompt follow-up checks. Hidden damage, composition, age, structural safety and residual service life **cannot** be established from a photo alone.

**My product work:** Problem discovery, exploratory stakeholder conversations, options and MVP prioritization, user flows, decision logic and a demonstrator.

**Status:** IHK project in progress; concept and interactive demonstrator, not a production-ready diagnosis or a validated environmental-impact calculation.
